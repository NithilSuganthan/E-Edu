Our Story

At Inventobots Academy, we believe that education should be more than just memorizing facts—it should be an adventure in discovery. Located in the heart of Nanganallur, Chennai, we are a premier educational hub dedicated to empowering the next generation of tech leaders and creative thinkers.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ignite curiosity and foster a growth mindset in kids and teens through hands-on learning. We aim to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world application by providing a supportive environment where students feel encouraged to explore, build, and innovate.

What We Do

Robotics & Electronics
Students dive into the world of mechanics and circuits, building functional robots from the ground up.
Coding & Programming
We teach the languages of the future, helping students develop logical thinking and computational skills.
STEM & Creative Thinking
Our curriculum focuses on 21st-century skills, encouraging students to solve complex problems.
Expert Academic Tuition
Personalized coaching for all subjects (CBSE and State Board), ensuring strong academic outcomes.
